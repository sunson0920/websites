# BLISS

Shot as a Windows XP screen recording. Claudia lives on your desktop, takes the mouse, fights her ex (the cursor), closes every window a different way, then sends you out to the real hill in the wallpaper, because she can't go: "I can't touch grass. You can."

The XP desktop drawn in code (Luna windows, the taskbar, balloons, the Welcome and Turn Off screens) over the real Bliss photograph, the real XP sounds placed in key, Midjourney loops moving inside the windows, and Claudia in Y2K high fashion: the Bliss gown, the cyber trench, the holo dancer, Start, silver night and the window dress.

- **Song:** 3:01 · Suno v6 · 130 BPM · E-flat major / C minor
- **Edit:** 44 shots · 9 chapters · 240 sung words on screen
- **Midjourney:** 138 prompts · 315 images · 81 plates · 35 Animate loops
- **Seedance 2.5:** 50 clips · 267 s
- **Made in:** about 19 hours · 21 notes from the director
