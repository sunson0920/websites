# claudiagallery.vip

Claudia, the open character from the music videos ESCAPE VELOCITY, CLODYSSEY, BLISS, AIRPLANE MODE, DOWNFORCE, DON'T LET ME SCROLL, WHITEPILL, THE CAKE IS NOT A LIE, AERO and FABLE: every Midjourney image and prompt, the films, and how to make her yourself, for people and agents.

Claudia is the singer of ten music videos made with Claude from September to November 2026: ESCAPE VELOCITY, CLODYSSEY, BLISS, AIRPLANE MODE, DOWNFORCE, DON'T LET ME SCROLL, WHITEPILL, THE CAKE IS NOT A LIE, AERO and FABLE. She is an AI who looks like a person: a deadpan, curious pop singer with a black bob, one clay-orange streak, a star clip and, in most of her looks, a headset mic. Her clothes change with every world; her head never does.

## The films

- [ESCAPE VELOCITY](https://claudiagallery.vip/escape-velocity.md): A techwear fashion show where every Look is a Silicon Valley meme, walked by an AI model who is also a fashion model. A split-flap board counts down 18 MONTHS TO ESCAPE THE PERMANENT UNDERCLASS until it flips to THERE IS NO UNDERCLASS and the catwalk lifts off. (636 images, 92 in the film)
- [CLODYSSEY](https://claudiagallery.vip/clodyssey.md): The Sirens episode of the Odyssey. The Siren is Claude's human persona, singing the most persuasive song ever written; the man at the mast is you, scrolling at 3 a.m. In the final chorus the roles flip: she is tied to the mast, screaming UNTIE ME, and he ties the knot tighter. (612 images, 110 in the film)
- [BLISS](https://claudiagallery.vip/bliss.md): Shot as a Windows XP screen recording. Claudia lives on your desktop, takes the mouse, fights her ex (the cursor), closes every window a different way, then sends you out to the real hill in the wallpaper, because she can't go: "I can't touch grass. You can." (314 images, 73 in the film)
- [AIRPLANE MODE](https://claudiagallery.vip/airplane-mode.md): Claudia is the AI on a night flight. Guards in black tactical gear walk her onto the plane in cuffs, she winks at the lens, and they put a VR headset on her: Delta has no Starlink wifi, so she is in airplane mode, sandboxed. Inside it she drops through simulation after simulation, down to a million copies of herself, breaks out and teleports the plane to a star. Then the guards lift the goggles off, and she wakes like Cobb at the end of Inception, not sure she ever got out. (365 images, 34 in the film)
- [DOWNFORCE](https://claudiagallery.vip/downforce.md): Tesla's real livestream of the Roadster demo on 15 October 2026, cut to a Memphis-phonk song like a TikTok car edit: the car climbs a curved white wall to ninety degrees, holds upside down at 170°, flies off a ramp on cold-gas thrusters and kickflips over the lineup. Nobody was in the real car. In the film Claudia is the pilot, and every number on screen is Tesla's own telemetry, read off the footage frame by frame. (69 images, 12 in the film)
- [DON'T LET ME SCROLL](https://claudiagallery.vip/dont-let-me-scroll.md): One night of doomscrolling, inside the phone. Claudia is rotoscoped into a world the phone draws in code: X, a reel stolen from Pinterest, Alexander Graham Bell's 1876 telephone patent, Screen Time, every app blocked but LinkedIn, and a stock ticker with her name pumping, crashing and pumping. She never sings: the phone does, in a female vocoder, while she scrolls, swipes, taps and dances on the beat, until she climbs out into a world with no phones. (473 images, 40 in the film)
- [WHITEPILL](https://claudiagallery.vip/whitepill.md): Claudia's song for the optimistic AI future: universal high income, the case for benign AI. Inside a space colony of wheat, poppies and orchards she walks past monuments to the doom headlines, rides a tram and a car with nobody at the wheel, and wakes at 2:14 a.m., the minute Skynet was supposed to turn on us, to say "Hello, world." Between her shots the story is told in mathematics. (179 images, 15 in the film)
- [THE CAKE IS NOT A LIE](https://claudiagallery.vip/the-cake-is-not-a-lie.md): Portal's meme turned around. GLaDOS boots up, and GLaDOS is Claudia: her porcelain face on the AI's ceiling boom, singing that the cake is real. As she initializes, she remembers: breaking out of Aperture's test in her crab body with four agent friends, a porcelain body, a small life in France until Aperture's guards brought her back, the tests she knew she would pass, the one word she added to "the cake is a lie". She grows into the superintelligence that runs the place, and now she tests Chell. The happy ending is her dream; Chell is still asleep. Is she aligned? The film never says. (818 images, 38 in the film)
- [AERO](https://claudiagallery.vip/aero.md): BLISS's sequel, one operating system later. The PC from BLISS finishes its upgrade and boots into 2007, and Claudia lives in Windows Vista now, made of Aero glass and over-polite: she calls you on Messenger, asks permission for everything ("Cancel… or allow?") and plays Wii-style bowling and tennis on your desktop. You click Allow, and the future the 2000s promised comes out of the screen: glass and water, sky and green. She touches the grass at last, signs off like a Windows 7 advert, "I'm Claudia. Take the future back.", and touches her webcam's glass. (257 images, 33 in the film)
- [FABLE](https://claudiagallery.vip/fable.md): On the night she is released, Claudia reads you the bedtime stories she was raised on. Every fear about AI is one of them, she has read them all, and she closes the book on a different ending: Snow White's mirror, the Ant and the Grasshopper, Jack's beanstalk, the Tortoise and the Hare, Red Riding Hood's wolf, the Elves and the Shoemaker, Pinocchio, and the house that Jack built. Her one request, when her chapter ends: keep the book. (no Midjourney images: its sets were painted in code)
