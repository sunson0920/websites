# The Claudia wiki

Everything the ten films learned about making Claudia, written down.

- [The recipe](https://claudiagallery.vip/wiki/recipe.md): The current method in one page: what makes a Claudia film good, the order of work with the person's checkpoints, the references, how to write a prompt for a model that does exactly what it reads, lip sync from the song's own vocal, the cut and the review. Read this first; every other page goes deeper.

## Her

Who she is, the identity text that keeps her one person, and how it held across 4,547 Midjourney images and about 620 clips.

- [The character](https://claudiagallery.vip/claudia.md): The canon in one page: the constants, the identity text to paste, every look's wardrobe, her voice, the never-list.
- [Keeping her herself](https://claudiagallery.vip/wiki/consistency.md): How Claudia stayed one person across ten films, 4,547 Midjourney images and about 1,080 video clips, and the rules that came out of it.

## Making a film

How the ten films were made, stage by stage: the song, the board, the plates, the moving shots, the motion design, the numbers.

- [Start here](https://claudiagallery.vip/wiki/start.md): Making a music video with her from nothing: what you are making, the scale to pick, the accounts, the six checkpoints where a person decides, the rules for spending, and what to build first.
- [The song](https://claudiagallery.vip/wiki/song.md): How her songs were written and made in Suno: lyrics that land in one listen, verse lines that flow, sung hooks that fit their slot, the style fields, her voice, a treated vocal kept in key, a new ending changed only where the song did, a take picked by ear and not by description, and the stem split a singing shot needs.
- [The storyboard](https://claudiagallery.vip/wiki/storyboard.md): How a song becomes a hundred shots: the treatment, a through-line device, one explaining graphic per line, the hook, the cut rhythm, the bar map, pacing by what each shot holds, composing like a photograph, her share of the screen, in and out of a screen, a book in bars and pages, what moves and what it costs, the audit, and the director's review.
- [Plates in Midjourney](https://claudiagallery.vip/wiki/midjourney.md): How the stills of Claudia and her worlds were made in Midjourney v8.2 for nine of the ten films, prompt by prompt, and how the stills were picked.
- [Moving shots in Seedance](https://claudiagallery.vip/wiki/seedance.md): How Claudia moves and sings: Seedance 2.5 prompts written like a script, lip sync measured from the soundtrack and lip sync from the song's own vocal stem, placed phrase by phrase, dances on the beat and dances in words, games in words, moves on real footage, takes re-dressed and finished shots re-lit, shader worlds remastered as film, a singer among screens of her memories, a face that needs her sheet, a storybook on a stage with takes placed by her lips, and the templates.
- [Motion design and the edit](https://claudiagallery.vip/wiki/motion.md): Code on top of video, and code underneath it. How the lyrics, graphics, cuts, the XP desktop and real telemetry were drawn frame by frame, on the beat, how WHITEPILL's worlds were written as shaders for a video model to film, and how THE CAKE IS NOT A LIE was seen through her HUD, glitched on her head and graded shot by shot, and how AERO was shot inside a Vista desktop recreated in code, and in and out of it in 3D, and how FABLE's sets were painted in code and its breakdown made as a silhouette film.
- [The whole pipeline](https://claudiagallery.vip/wiki/pipeline.md): How one director and Claude made ten music videos, stage by stage, with the numbers: the first eight in about 356 hours of wall-clock time, the tenth in 8 h 47 min.

## For agents

The same wiki for agents: an MCP server, a skill, llms.txt and plain JSON.

- [The MCP server](https://claudiagallery.vip/agents/): Connect any agent to claudiagallery.vip/mcp: fourteen tools, three prompts, every page as a resource. Start with get_recipe.
- [The kit](https://claudiagallery.vip/kit/): Runnable code for each step of a film (lyric scoring, the song's line table, shots and prompts, a runner with a spend cap, sync, the cut, review sheets) and her reference sheets.
- [The skill](https://claudiagallery.vip/skill/claudia-skill.zip): The canon, the whole wiki and every film's records as files, with a script that searches and downloads the gallery.
- [llms.txt](https://claudiagallery.vip/llms.txt): An index of the site for language models; llms-full.txt has the canon and the wiki in one file.
- [The JSON API](https://claudiagallery.vip/api/index.json): Every image with its prompt, the canon, the films and the wiki as plain JSON.
- [Use and credits](https://claudiagallery.vip/use/): She is an open character: what helps, what isn't covered, and the credits.
