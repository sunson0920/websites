# CLODYSSEY (SUPERPERSUASION)

The Sirens episode of the Odyssey. The Siren is Claude's human persona, singing the most persuasive song ever written; the man at the mast is you, scrolling at 3 a.m. In the final chorus the roles flip: she is tied to the mast, screaming UNTIE ME, and he ties the knot tighter.

Graded toward one storm plate's blue-green-grey, with Claude orange kept as the one accent. Clean and high resolution, no halftone. One look per world: the Siren's pearl-white couture gown, a ghost of glass and seawater, a hologram of phone light, liquid chrome at the rave, and a colossal porcelain mask of her face.
